## English

**PEACE Biomedical & Engineering Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. — office monitoring acknowledgment**

PEACE Biomedical & Engineering Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. uses the TeamPulse monitoring software on company computers. During office hours it keeps a record of work activity for timesheets, payroll and work evidence. It **never** records what you type, your messages, your camera or microphone, or anything outside working time. What is recorded, who can see it, and your rights were explained in the team meeting, and you can ask HR / Administration (admin@medtec24.com) about any of it at any time.

Records are kept only as long as needed: screenshots 30 days, other activity 12 months, payroll and HR records 7 years (or as the law requires). You can see your own data in the app at any time, and no decision about you is made from monitoring data alone.

**Consent.** By selecting "I consent", you confirm that you are aware of the office monitoring software and agree to the recording of your work activity on company computers during office hours. If you have questions before consenting, please speak to HR / Administration (admin@medtec24.com).
