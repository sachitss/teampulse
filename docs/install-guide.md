# Installing TeamPulse on your laptop and phone

TeamPulse is a web app you can **install**. Once installed, it opens in its own window with its own icon, like any other app. Updates arrive automatically; there is nothing to download from an app store.

Open the address PEACE gave you (for example **https://teampulse.peace.com.np**) and sign in with the email invitation you received.

## Windows or Mac laptop

**Chrome or Microsoft Edge**
1. Open the TeamPulse address and sign in.
2. Click the **install icon** at the right end of the address bar (a small screen with a down arrow). Or open the **⋮** menu and choose **Install TeamPulse**. In Edge it is **… → Apps → Install TeamPulse**.
3. Click **Install**. TeamPulse appears in the Start menu or Applications and can be pinned to the taskbar or dock.

**Safari on Mac (macOS Sonoma or newer):** **File → Add to Dock**.

## Android phone

1. Open the address in **Chrome** and sign in.
2. Tap **⋮ → Install app** (on some phones it says **Add to Home screen**).
3. Tap **Install**. The TeamPulse icon appears with your other apps.

## iPhone or iPad

1. Open the address in **Safari** and sign in.
2. Tap the **Share** button (a square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down, tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

You need iOS 16.4 or newer. Other browsers on iPhone may not offer this option, so use Safari.

## What the phone app is for

- Starting and stopping your work timer, breaks, and switching tasks
- Checking your timesheet and submitting your month
- For managers: approving time and timesheets

The phone app **does not monitor anything**: no screenshots, no app tracking. Activity recording happens only on company laptops, and only as described in the notice you agreed to.

## Two-factor sign-in (HR and admin only)

HR and admin accounts must use an authenticator app, such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or Authy. The first time you sign in, scan the QR code TeamPulse shows. After that, enter the 6-digit code each time.

## Trouble?

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| No install option | Make sure you opened the **https://** address in Chrome or Edge (laptop or Android) or Safari (iPhone) |
| "You are offline" | TeamPulse needs internet. Your data is never stored on the device |
| Invitation link expired | Ask the admin to send a new invitation, or use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page |
| Code rejected (two-factor) | Make sure the time on your phone is set automatically, then try the newest code |
